# DWIM Logic — 14-Day Launch Sprint

Goal: first paid job in 14 days, three paying clients in 45. Speed over polish.

## Ground rules
- **Geography:** pitch government and municipal AV partners **outside Florida only.** Do not contact South Florida AV or video firms. Keep every public page free of city names and addresses.
- **Lead with agencies and brands.** They buy fastest. Government is a slower second track through partners.
- **One free first cutdown** per new agency partner, capped at 60 seconds of finished video, to remove all risk. Nothing else is free.
- **Fixed prices, written down before you pitch.** Final rate card is below.
- Lead with senior craft and speed. Mention AI as how it is fast. Disclose AI use in every contract.

## Day-by-day

**Days 1–2: get findable and quotable**
- Register the entity name and a business email on dwimlogic.com. Publish the site (use a privacy-protected address, no city).
- Build one 30–60 second **proof reel**: same brief, before (traditional timeline) and after (DWIM). Use your own footage or the Lower Third Studio output.
- Finish one PDF one-pager: offers, rate card, turnaround, how to send a brief.
- Create one intake form (the structured brief): goal, deliverables, formats, deadline, reference links, brand assets, approver, revision contact.

**Days 3–5: first 25 messages (warm list)**
- Pull 25 people from your own network: producers, agency owners, post supervisors, former students now in the industry, station contacts. Send the Agency message below, personally, one by one.
- Offer the free first cutdown and a 15-minute call.

**Days 6–8: next 25 plus partner outreach**
- 25 more warm contacts, brands this time (marketing leads, in-house video teams).
- **Government partner track (outside Florida):** build a list of 20 municipal AV integrators and government-access TV vendors in other states. Segments to search: council-chamber AV integrators, PEG and government-access TV departments, and resellers of live government production systems (for example Broadcast Pix's GovTV ecosystem). Public examples of in-house city channels include Garland, TX and Rochester, NH. Use the Partner message below.

**Days 9–12: deliver the first free cutdown, ask for the paid job**
- Deliver within 48 hours of receiving assets. Send a short loom-style walkthrough with it.
- Same day: send the quote for the next job. Ask for a small standing monthly volume.

**Days 13–14: review and adjust**
- Count: messages sent, replies, calls, quotes, paid jobs. Keep what produced replies and cut what did not.
- Line up three freelancers (editor, motion, audio) with written rates, so capacity is ready before you need it.

## Delivery standard (every job)
- Loudness and levels, safe areas, spelling, caption accuracy, spec check.
- AI disclosure line in the delivery note; rights log attached.
- Written release for any voice or likeness.
- Two revision rounds with timestamped notes. A third round is a change order.

## What would stall this
- Waiting for a perfect site. The page is good enough to send now.
- No proof reel. Build it first.
- No bench. Line up three freelancers before the second client.
- Underpricing. Do not drop below the rate card to close a first job; give the free cutdown instead.
