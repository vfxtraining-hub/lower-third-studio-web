# THE ARMY OF ONE — Epic Reel Plan

An AI agency's proof reel should be made by an AI agency. Every picture, sound and graphic is generated, and every choice is a senior producer's. This reel is the portfolio, the pitch and the proof in one piece.

**Supersedes** the filmed approach in `PROOF_REEL.md`. Its "two clocks" graphics (`reel-graphics.html`) survive as the closing making-of timer.

## 1. The idea

Thirty years of craft, then one person commanding an entire production.

A lone producer stands in an empty soundstage at dawn. Cut through three decades of tools in match cuts, each one handled by the same hands. Then he raises a hand and the stage assembles itself: lights, cranes, tracks, a crew of silhouettes made of light, timelines hanging in the air like ribbons. The frame multiplies into every format a client needs. He lowers his hand. Everything freezes. One red tally light comes on. Title.

**The line the film proves:** *A one-person army with a 30-year bench.*

**Why it earns its place.** It shows range (epic scale, tactile craft, graphics, sound), shows taste (restraint, motivated camera, one accent color), and ends with a making-of timer that states how long this film took to make. The reel is the demo.

## 2. Format

- **Master:** 75 seconds, 2.39:1 letterbox inside 1920×1080, 24fps, stereo mix at −14 LUFS
- **Cutdowns:** 30s, 15s, and 9:16 and 1:1 versions
- **Website loop:** a 6-second silent hero loop from the best wide shot
- **Disclosure card at the end:** "Made entirely with AI under senior direction." This is required, not optional. It is also the pitch.

## 3. Producer's laws (the taste layer)

1. **One accent color.** Ink black, warm tungsten, and one tally red that appears in every shot.
2. **No faces unless earned.** Backs, silhouettes, hands, profiles. Show a face in at most two shots, and only if the likeness is the principal's own, with a reference image and consent.
3. **Cut on motion.** Every cut hides a move. No dissolves.
4. **Hold the wide.** The hero crane shot gets 4 full seconds. Epic needs room to breathe.
5. **Motivated light.** Every source is visible or implied. No flat video lighting.
6. **No text inside generated frames.** All type is built in post.
7. **Reject the mush.** Anything that morphs, warps hands, or melts geometry is a retake, not a fix.
8. **Silence is a weapon.** Drop the music for the 0:52 freeze.

## 4. Style bible (paste into every prompt)

> Shot on 35mm anamorphic, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, deep ink-black shadows, warm tungsten practical lights, a single small red tally light as the only saturated color, cinematic restraint, no text, no logos.

Negative guide: no cartoon look, no neon cyberpunk, no lens flare overload, no text, no watermarks, no extra fingers.

## 7. Music and sound

- **Music:** instrumental, about 100 BPM, minimal pulse, strings and sub bass, builds across Movements 3–4, drops to silence at 0:52, returns for one resolved hit on the title. Generate 3 candidates and choose the best, or pick a licensed track from the catalog.
- **Sound design:** room tone, footsteps, splicer click, deck button thunk, relay clacks as lights snap on, low trailer-style risers, one clean red-light "tick" at 0:56.
- **Mix:** dialogue-first, music ducked 6 dB under VO, −14 LUFS stereo.
