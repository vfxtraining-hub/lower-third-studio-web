# DWIM Logic — Business Plan v0.1

*Do What I Mean.* An AI-native production agency built on 30+ years of real production and post-production craft.

> Market research and competitor analysis: see `RESEARCH.md`. Animated site: `site.html` (artifact version); `index.html` is the earlier plain draft.

## 1. Thesis

Most "AI video" shops are prompt operators with no production discipline. Most traditional shops are priced and staffed for a pre-AI world. DWIM Logic sits in the gap:

- **Craft:** three decades of directing, producing, editing, grading, graphics, sound, delivery, and teaching. We know what "broadcast-ready" means and what a client will actually accept.
- **Leverage:** AI + MCP-connected tooling (NLE automation, generation, graphics, captioning, versioning) so one senior producer runs the output of a whole department.
- **Result:** Better, faster, cheaper. All three, because the senior human is the quality gate and the pipeline does the volume.

**Positioning line:** *A one-person army with a 30-year bench.*

## 2. The "man army" model

| Layer | Who/what | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Creative + QC | Yan (senior producer/editor) | Brief, taste, final approval, client relationship |
| Pipeline | MCP-connected agents (Resolve, Eddie, Higgsfield, Artlist, Gamma, custom Studio tools) | Rough cuts, graphics, versions, captions, gen assets, delivery |
| Surge | Vetted freelancers (per project) | Scale for on-set, specialty VFX, or volume spikes |

Rule: **nothing ships without senior-human QC.** That's the moat vs. pure-AI competitors.

## 3. Low-hanging fruit (launch order)

Sell what is already built or fastest to deliver, to the buyers who already feel the pain.

### Wave 1 — start selling now (days to first dollar)
1. **Agency/producer overflow (white-label).** Other shops need capacity: rough cuts, cutdowns, versioning, captions. Fast sales cycle, repeat revenue, no brand risk for us. *Easiest first client type.*
2. **Social cutdown & versioning packs.** One long asset → 10–30 platform versions (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, captions, hooks). Priced per pack.
3. **Lower-third & broadcast graphics packages.** Already productized via Lower Third Studio (alpha PNG sequences, Resolve-ready, Fusion switcher). Sell as per-roster packages to municipalities, events, corporate, broadcasters.

### Wave 2 — within 30–60 days
4. **Government/institutional video:** meeting recaps, public-info spots, anniversary packages, accessibility captions. Recurring retainers, procurement-friendly small contracts.
5. **Brand explainers & product spots** with AI-assisted generation plus real footage where it matters.
6. **Localization:** captions, translation, AI voice-over versions.

### Wave 3 — 60–120 days
7. **Broadcast/streaming post support** (graphics, versioning, QC, delivery specs).
8. **Retainer "content engine"**: monthly volume with fixed price.
9. **Training/workshops** (30 years of teaching is an asset): "AI + MCP for editors/producers" for teams and schools. Also a lead generator.

## 5. Go-to-market

1. **Warm list first:** 30 years of contacts. Past clients, students now working in the industry, producers, station and agency people. Target: 50 personal messages in week 1, offering a *free first cutdown* to prove it.
2. **Proof assets:** 3 before/after reels (same brief: traditional timeline vs. DWIM timeline), plus a one-page "how we're 3x faster without cutting corners."
3. **Productized landing page** (this repo's `index.html`) with Calendly-style "send us a brief" and a 48-hour quote promise.
4. **Local government outreach:** short pilot offer (e.g., anniversary or meeting-recap package) leveraging existing civic work.
5. **Agency partnerships:** white-label rate card, NDA-friendly, invisible to their client.
6. **Content:** weekly build-in-public clips of the pipeline working (the "impossible" angle). Teaching background makes this natural.

## 6. Operations and risk

- **Tool stack:** DaVinci Resolve (+ scripting/MCP), Eddie AI, Higgsfield, Artlist, Gamma, Lower Third Studio, plus Claude as the orchestrator.
- **Rights and licensing:** use licensed/commercially cleared generation and music only; keep a rights log per project. Disclose AI use per client contract.
- **Quality:** a one-page QC checklist on every delivery (audio levels, safe areas, spelling, caption accuracy, spec compliance).
- **Likeness/voice:** written consent for any cloned voice or likeness. No exceptions.
- **Concentration risk:** no single client above 30% of revenue.
- **Key-person risk:** document pipelines as runnable playbooks so freelancers can plug in.
